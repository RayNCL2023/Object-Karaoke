## Object vs Object battle
Two different people each snap a photo of an object. Both objects come alive as characters (googly eyes, cut-out, their own designed ElevenLabs voices) and rap battle EACH OTHER while the humans watch. Everyone in the room votes and judges on their phones (round votes + final verdict), and the crowd's votes decide the winner.

Notes for later:
- Stage: two characters facing each other (left/right), alternating verses, each reacting to the other's lines.
- Phones: the two "trainers" who snapped can hype their object (cheer buttons) but don't rap; judges vote per round.
- Reuse: PvP turn loop (lib/pvp.mjs), audience votes (lib/audience.mjs), character pipeline (lib/duet.mjs buildSong / voice design / Music API).
- Memory: objects remember who beat them.
