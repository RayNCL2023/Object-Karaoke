# Social posts (post after the event)

## LinkedIn - Kaif

Yesterday my water bottle called me "our kid" and gave my singing 41 out of 100. Fair, to be honest.

At Hacktoberfest Hack Day Manchester, Debanjan Ray and I built **dissPICable** in a single day: snap any object with your phone, it comes alive as a cheeky Mancunian character, sings a karaoke duet with you, then roasts your performance.

How it works:
🎤 Gemma 4 (via the Gemini API) looks at the photo, invents the character and writes the song
🗣️ ElevenLabs gives every object its own voice, and its speech-to-text marks your singing
🧠 OpenLoft's NMAFC memory means objects remember you, so the same mug keeps its name and its grudges
🥁 Tone.js plays the backing beat, and your phone's camera, mic and vibration are the controller

All the code was written on the day, it's open source (MIT), and it needs no installs to run. Completely pointless, and I'm proper proud of it.

Massive thanks to the Open Source Society UoM and MLH for running a brilliant day, to the sponsors and mentors, and to every judge who let us borrow their stuff.
