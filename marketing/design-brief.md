# Pitch deck brief - 6 slides

## Brand notes
- **Name:** Duet Anything (always two words, both capitalised).
- **Feel:** a dark stage just before the spotlight hits. Playful, loud, a bit daft, never messy.
- **Palette:**
  - Stage black `#0d0d12` (background)
  - Curtain grey `#1c1c26` (cards, panels)
  - Spotlight white `#f5f2ea` (text)
  - **Hot accent: Mic Red `#ff3d5a`** (one per slide, for the key word or button only)
  - Bee gold `#ffc629`, used tiny, only for the bee nod
- **Type:** a chunky rounded display face for headlines (Fredoka or Baloo 2, Google Fonts), Inter for body. Headlines huge, max 6 words.
- **Manchester bee nod:** a small worker bee wearing headphones as the mic dot in the logo, or a hexagon pattern faint in a corner. One per slide max.
- **Motion (if live):** fade and rise only, no spins. Lyrics-style text that lights up word by word on slide 1.
- **Imagery:** real photos of everyday objects (mug, lanyard, bottle) with cartoon eyes and a mouth, on the dark stage.

## Slide by slide

### 1. Title
- **Headline:** Duet Anything
- **Sub:** Snap any object. It sings a duet with you. Then it roasts you.
- **Visual:** a mug with cartoon eyes under a red spotlight, singing into a mic.
- **Footer:** Kaif Nadeem + Debanjan Ray · Hacktoberfest Hack Day Manchester · 11 Oct 2026

### 2. The fun (problem)
- **Headline:** Karaoke needs a partner
- **Copy:** Your mates won't sing. Your mug will. Everything on your desk has a personality, and an opinion on your singing.
- **Visual:** a lonely person with a mic; a lanyard looking smug next to them.

### 3. How it works
- **Headline:** Snap. Sing. Get roasted.
- **Three steps with icons:**
  1. **Snap** any object with your phone
  2. **Sing** your lines, the object sings back in its own voice
  3. **Get roasted** and scored out of 100. It remembers you next time.
- **Visual:** phone, then stage, then score with confetti.

### 4. Tech + 8 tracks
- **Headline:** Serious tech, daft result
- **Left column (stack):** Gemma 4 via Gemini API (character, song, judge) · ElevenLabs voices + speech-to-text · OpenLoft NMAFC memory · Tone.js beat · phone camera, mic, vibration · Node, zero installs
- **Right column (tracks, one line each):** Gemma 4 · Open-Source AI · ElevenLabs · OpenLoft · Music · Best UI · Manchester Madness · Physical World
- **Footer:** Open source, MIT, all code written today.
